The yacht (assets/models/yacht-b04.glb): generated with Tripo, an AI model service, from a picture that the owner of this game supplied, then reduced in Blender. The terms of that service for a published game have not been checked yet; the model is on this test page for a phone test.
The llama and the emperor (assets/models/llama-anim.glb, emperor.glb): characters of the owner of this game, generated with Tripo from his own character sheets and reduced in Blender; the emperor shows the owner himself, with his consent. The terms of that service for a published game have not been checked yet.
The king and the little captain of the voyages (assets/models/people/king.glb, jack.glb): characters of the client this game is made for, generated with Tripo from two character sheets that the owner of this game supplied, and reduced in Blender. The terms of that service for a published game have not been checked yet.
The picture on the sail of the jukung (assets/textures/sail-emblem.webp): cut from a picture that the owner of this game supplied as the artwork for that sail.
The crew (assets/models/people, f01 to f14): figures of real people who agreed to be in these games, generated with Tripo from photographs and reduced in Blender; the game names nobody. The terms of that service for a published game have not been checked yet.
The four other boats (assets/models/boats): made from the owner's own concept sheets with Tripo and reduced in Blender. The terms of that service for a published game have not been checked yet.
The dragon world (assets/models/dragon-world.glb): a model of the owner of this game, generated with Tripo and reduced in Blender. The terms of that service for a published game have not been checked yet.
The code, the pictures drawn in the page, the sounds and the icons were made for this game.
